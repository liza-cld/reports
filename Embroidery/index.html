5.1Minimum stitched width

Each size-dependent criterion scales with the stitched width. The bars show the smallest logo width at which each criterion is met; bars that end beyond the 4 in line fail at 4 in.

5.2Observations

  1. Tagline. “PERSONALIZED GIFTING MADE EASY” is the only element below the limits. At 4 in its letters are 3.58 mm tall, 56% of the 6.35 mm minimum, and its strokes are 0.36–0.41 mm against a 0.50 mm minimum.
  2. ™ mark. 1.5 mm tall with 0.08 mm strokes in both artworks. It is below the 2 mm detail size at any practical embroidery width and is normally removed or enlarged when the design is digitized.
  3. Hidden artwork. The tagline file contains a second, identical copy of the GIVEE wordmark (5 shapes, same colour) placed exactly behind the visible one. It does not show in print, but if the vector file is imported directly into digitizing software it may appear as a duplicate set of objects.
  4. Brush texture. The “Select” lettering and the two swashes have rough brush edges with fine detail under 0.5 mm. These details are smoothed during digitizing; the stroke widths above are measured on the letter bodies. The swashes (typical width 0.40 mm at 4 in) suit a running or bean stitch rather than satin.
  5. Colour. Both artworks use two flat colours: near-black #221F20 and green #528563. No gradients, blends or embedded images were found.
Comments

All values are measured on the vector outlines of the supplied EPS files, scaled so the full artwork width equals the stitched width (1052.0 pt of artwork = 4.00 in). Letter height is the height of each letter outline; the smallest letter of each text line is reported. Stroke width is the typical thickness of each letter, measured along the middle of its strokes; tapered tips are excluded. Colours are sampled from the rendered artwork. Fully hidden shapes are excluded from scoring and listed in 5.2.

The thresholds in this assessment are common industry values. Every rule is configurable: limits, stitched sizes and the set of criteria can be adjusted to your specific requirements, such as fabric, stitch type or your production partner's specifications, and the assessment re-run against them.
